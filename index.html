§1. Research

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Vision and Image Processing Laboratory at Duke University, working with Professor Sina Farsiu. I came to medical AI from computer engineering, and to computer engineering from physics.1

My research develops AI methods for medicine that are mathematically grounded and built around foundation models. Most of my work concerns learning from imperfect biomedical data: semi- and unsupervised learning, transfer learning, and multimodal learning when domains shift and labels are noisy or scarce. Recent work includes mixture-of-experts for semi-supervised histopathology segmentation, co-training the Segment Anything Model with conventional networks on unlabeled images, and source-free domain adaptation.
