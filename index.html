I. Introduction

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Vision and Image Processing Laboratory at Duke University, working with Professor Sina Farsiu. Before Duke I studied computer engineering at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology, where I graduated as valedictorian, and before that I studied physics.†

Much of my research can be read as variations on a single objective. Given a small labeled set DL​ and a large unlabeled set DU​, one trains a model fθ​ by minimizing

L(θ)=few labelsE(x,y)∼DL​​ℓ(fθ​(x),y)​​+λmany imagesEx∼DU​​Ω(fθ​,x)​​
(1)

where the second term asks the model to be consistent on images nobody has annotated. The hard part is that DU​ is rarely clean: stains vary, scanners differ, and the labels in DL​ are noisy. My papers propose ways to make (1) behave under these conditions, using mixture-of-experts [7], co-training with foundation models [2], hierarchical distillation [9] and disentangled representations [10].
