§1 Research

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Biomedical Engineering at Duke University, advised by Prof. Sina Farsiu. I came to medical AI from computer engineering, and to computer engineering from physics.

My research develops AI-powered medical solutions, built on mathematically grounded modeling and foundation-model-driven designs. Most of my work concerns learning from imperfect biomedical data: semi- and unsupervised learning, transfer learning, and deep multimodal learning under domain shift and noisy or scarce labels.

Keywords: semi-supervised learning; foundation models; domain adaptation; mixture-of-experts; histopathology; ultrasound and MRI segmentation.
